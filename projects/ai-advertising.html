AI + Advertising

AI-Powered Advertising Campaigns

Creative marketing projects exploring how generative AI can be used to develop advertising concepts, visuals, and video while maintaining human authenticity.

Focus Areas

AI Tools, Creative Strategy, Campaign Development

Skills

Generative AI, Copywriting, Visual Ideation, Brand Strategy

The Exploration

As generative AI tools become increasingly accessible, marketers face a critical question: how do we leverage AI to enhance creativity and efficiency without losing authenticity or human connection? This project explored that intersection through hands-on experimentation.

The Process

  • Tool Evaluation: Tested several generative AI platforms to understand capabilities and limitations.
  • Conceptual Ideation: Used AI to generate campaign concepts, headlines, and creative directions based on strategic briefs.
  • Visual Development: Explored AI image generation for mood boards and visual concepts.
  • Video & Motion: Experimented with AI video generation to support campaign storytelling and content production.
  • Human Iteration: Refined AI outputs through human creativity, strategic thinking, and brand alignment.

Key Insights

AI is a Creative Partner

AI works best as a brainstorming partner and production accelerator, not a replacement for human strategy.

Authenticity Requires Direction

Specific, human-centered briefs produce stronger results than generic prompts.

Human Touch Matters

Audiences can sense when content lacks intention and emotional resonance.

Practical Applications

1. Rapid Concept Development

Use AI to generate multiple campaign directions quickly before refining the strongest concept.

2. Copywriting & Messaging

Draft variations of headlines and calls-to-action, then edit for voice and strategy.

3. Visual Mood Boards

Generate creative inspiration and mood boards to explore aesthetics before production.

4. Content Production

Accelerate video and motion work with AI-assisted editing and effects under human oversight.

5. Personalization at Scale

Use AI to tailor messaging and visuals for audience segments without losing brand consistency.

My Generative AI Video Process

I created my generative AI videos by combining creative strategy, storyboarding, visual direction, AI generation, sound, and video editing. I approached each project like a real advertising production, starting with the idea and building each scene intentionally from concept to final video.

01. Concept & Storyboard

I began by developing the creative concept and mapping out the story through a storyboard. I planned each scene, including the setting, characters, action, dialogue, camera angle, and overall purpose within the advertisement.

02. Visual Development

Using Nano Banana Pro, I developed the individual visual assets needed for each scene. I refined my prompts to control details such as composition, lighting, environment, character appearance, and visual style while working toward consistency across the video.

03. AI Video Generation

I brought the visuals to life using Google Flow, experimenting with different camera movements and cinematic techniques. I planned shots such as establishing shots, wide shots, medium shots, close-ups, tracking shots, and point-of-view perspectives to make the final video feel intentionally directed rather than like a collection of AI-generated clips.

04. Voiceover & Audio

I used ElevenLabs to create the voiceover, selecting a voice and delivery that matched the tone of the advertisement. I then incorporated music and sound effects to build emotion, reinforce the story, and make the final piece feel more like a finished commercial.

05. Editing & Post-Production

Finally, I brought the generated footage and audio together through editing. I selected the strongest clips, arranged them according to the storyboard, adjusted pacing and transitions, synchronized the voiceover, and refined the final sequence.

The process allowed me to explore how generative AI can support traditional advertising and storytelling, while still requiring human decisions around strategy, visual direction, narrative, pacing, and creative execution.
