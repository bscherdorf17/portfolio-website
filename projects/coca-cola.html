Consumer Insights

Coca-Cola Marketing Consulting Project

Team-based marketing strategy project focused on consumer insights, brand positioning, and growth opportunities for an iconic global brand.

Project Type

Marketing Consulting, Strategy

Skills

Consumer Research, Market Analysis, Brand Strategy

The Brief

This consulting project challenged our team to develop a strategic growth plan for Coca-Cola by conducting deep consumer research, analyzing market trends, and identifying opportunities to strengthen brand connection and drive business growth.

Research & Analysis

  • Consumer Research: Conducted surveys and interviews to understand motivations, brand perception, and purchasing behavior.
  • Competitive Analysis: Analyzed competing beverage brands and market share dynamics.
  • Trend Analysis: Examined sustainability, health, and personalization trends affecting consumer behavior.
  • Brand Audit: Evaluated Coca-Cola's positioning and marketing mix across channels and markets.

Key Findings

Emotional Connection

Coca-Cola's strongest brand equity comes from emotional connection and nostalgia, which continues to shape brand loyalty.

Generational Shift

Younger consumers prioritize sustainability and health, creating pressure to evolve the brand's offerings and story.

Personalization Opportunity

Consumers increasingly expect personalized experiences, which present new growth opportunities.

Strategic Recommendations

1. Evolve Brand Storytelling

Balance brand heritage with modern narratives that reflect contemporary values and cultural moments.

2. Expand Healthier Options

Invest in low-sugar and functional beverages to match changing consumer preferences.

3. Sustainability Leadership

Lean into environmental commitments as a central part of the brand identity.

4. Personalization & Digital

Build personalized marketing experiences and direct-to-consumer channels that create deeper engagement.

5. Multicultural Marketing

Develop culturally specific campaigns that speak authentically to diverse audiences.
