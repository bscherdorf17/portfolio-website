Brand Strategy

Strengthening Fordham University Campus Identity Through Strategic Swag & Promotion

A marketing consulting project focused on strengthening student connection to Fordham's brand through merchandise and promotion strategy.

Focus Areas

Brand Strategy, Student Engagement, Merchandise

Outcome

Strategic recommendations for campus swag and brand promotion initiatives

The Challenge

Fordham University sought to strengthen student connection to the university's brand and culture. The challenge was to identify how merchandise and promotional strategies could serve as tangible touchpoints that reinforce Fordham's identity and build belonging among the student body.

The Approach

  • Brand Audit: Analyzed Fordham's existing visual identity, brand guidelines, and current merchandise offerings.
  • Student Research: Conducted surveys and interviews with students to understand what drives engagement with university brands and why they wear/use campus merchandise.
  • Competitive Analysis: Studied merchandise strategies at peer institutions to identify best practices and opportunities.
  • Strategy Development: Created actionable recommendations for merchandise design, distribution, and promotional tactics.

Key Insights

Authenticity Matters

Students are more likely to wear and use merchandise that feels genuine to the university's culture and personal identity.

Quality Over Quantity

Premium, thoughtfully designed merchandise creates a stronger emotional connection than mass-produced items.

Community-Driven Design

Involving student input in design and merchandise selection increases adoption and creates ownership.

Recommendations

1. Premium Design Strategy

Develop a cohesive design language that reflects Fordham's heritage while feeling contemporary and appealing to current students.

2. Limited Editions & Exclusivity

Create seasonal or limited-edition drops that build anticipation and give merchandise a sense of value.

3. Student Collaboration

Partner with student design clubs and organizations to co-create merchandise and build community investment.

4. Multi-Channel Distribution

Offer merchandise through campus pop-ups, online stores, and student organizations to increase visibility.

5. Storytelling & Context

Share the story behind each design so merchandise reflects Fordham values and campus culture.

Impact & Takeaways

This project reinforced the power of merchandise as a brand-building tool when approached strategically. By centering student voice, prioritizing quality, and connecting merchandise to deeper brand values, universities can strengthen campus culture and identity.
