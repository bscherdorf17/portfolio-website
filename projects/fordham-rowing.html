Social Media

Fordham Rowing TikTok

Developed social media strategy and content creation for Fordham University Rowing, generating 2.5M+ total views.

Results

2.5M+ total views and 22.6K+ total likes

Skills

Social Media Strategy, Content Creation, Video Production

The Opportunity

Fordham University Rowing sought to build awareness and recruit talent through social media. TikTok presented a unique opportunity to reach prospective student-athletes where they spend their time and where authentic, engaging content can resonate quickly.

The Strategy

  • Audience Research: Identified target audience and their content preferences on TikTok.
  • Content Pillars: Developed themes around behind-the-scenes, athlete spotlights, training, team culture, and competitive highlights.
  • Trend Integration: Incorporated trending audio and formats to maximize reach while maintaining an authentic team voice.
  • Consistent Production: Established a content calendar and regular posting schedule to build momentum.

Content Themes

🎬 Behind-the-Scenes

Day-in-the-life vlogs and candid team moments that humanize the athletes.

⭐ Athlete Spotlights

Personal stories and motivations behind each rower's journey.

💪 Training & Fitness

Workout tips and training insights that highlight the hard work behind the sport.

🤝 Team Culture

Celebrations and bonding moments that build community and belonging.

🏆 Competitive Highlights

Race recaps and impressive athletic moments that inspire and engage viewers.

Key Results

2.5M+ Views

Reached more than 2.5 million total views across the TikTok content library.

22.6K+ Likes

Built strong engagement with more than 22,600 total likes across the account.

Authentic Storytelling

The most successful content was raw and authentic, highlighting real moments over polished branding.
