Product Marketing

Topcon Healthcare Product Marketing

Product marketing work involving competitive positioning, value propositions, clinical trial data organization, and marketing materials for ophthalmology technology.

Industry

Healthcare, Medical Technology

Skills

Product Positioning, Clinical Data, B2B Marketing

The Challenge

Topcon Healthcare needed to position an innovative ophthalmology technology in a competitive market. The challenge was to translate clinical research and technical specifications into compelling marketing messages that resonate with healthcare professionals.

My Contributions

  • Competitive Analysis: Researched competing products and identified differentiation opportunities.
  • Value Proposition Development: Worked with product and clinical teams to articulate key benefits and outcomes.
  • Clinical Data Organization: Reviewed data and synthesized findings into accessible summaries.
  • Marketing Collateral: Developed product briefs and comparison materials for sales and presentations.

Key Insights

Science + Simplicity

Complex clinical data is most compelling when explained clearly and translated for varied audiences.

Decision-Making Criteria

Healthcare professionals prioritize outcomes, ease of use, training requirements, and ROI.

Regulatory Alignment

Medical device marketing requires careful compliance without sacrificing strong messaging.

Approach to B2B Healthcare Marketing

1. Evidence-Based Messaging

All claims were grounded in clinical evidence and research.

2. Multiple Audience Segments

Messaging was tailored to clinicians, administrators, and technical stakeholders.

3. Clear ROI Story

Emphasized efficiency gains, patient outcomes, and cost-effectiveness.

4. Compliance-Ready Materials

Developed materials that met ethical and regulatory requirements.
